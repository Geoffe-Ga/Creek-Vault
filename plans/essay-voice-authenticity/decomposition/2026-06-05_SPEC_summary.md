# Essay Voice Authenticity — Decomposition Summary

Generated by the `spec-decomposition` skill on 2026-06-05 from a spoken problem report
(not a written SPEC). The owner observed that drafted essays do not sound like him and
traced three root causes. This file restates the problem, defines one epic, sequences its
child issues tracer-code style, and records the open decisions.

## Problem statement (owner's words, distilled)

> 1. My most public-facing work leans heavily on **references and quotes** from source
>    material my ideas touch. That pattern is prevalent in public work and rare in private
>    writing — but the voice model never picked it up. Voice authority should be **weighted by
>    the intended audience of a Fragment**: a public-facing Fragment should carry more weight
>    over the stylistic and content patterns that shape drafted output.
> 2. Many voice exemplars come from **AI conversations** and are therefore often AI's writing,
>    not mine. When ingesting AI chatbot conversations, the **human turns are self-written** and
>    the **AI turns are AI-assisted** — they must be attributed separately, and AI turns must
>    never train the voice proxy.
> 3. I doubt the **final lint pass for AI mannerisms** is actually executed faithfully. Debug it.

## Current-state findings (grounded in code)

- **Audience weight is unused.** `Fragment.privacy_tier` (`OPEN` / `PERSONAL` / `INTIMATE`,
  `creek/models.py`) is the de-facto audience axis, but voice generation only uses it as a
  *binary* gate (`INTIMATE` excluded in `creek/generate/voice.py::_eligible_register`). `OPEN`
  and `PERSONAL` fragments are ranked identically. `Fragment.representativeness` is stored but
  never consumed. No graduated audience-authority multiplier exists.
- **Citation/quotation density is unmeasured.** `VoicePatternExtractor`
  (`creek/generate/voice.py`) extracts sentence/paragraph structure, transitions, metaphors,
  rhetorical moves, vocabulary, and punctuation — but has **no** citation/quotation/reference
  detector. The exact pattern the owner names is invisible to the model.
- **AI chat turns leak into the voice corpus.** `ClaudeIngestor._build_fragment`
  (`creek/ingest/claude.py`) and the ChatGPT ingestor (`creek/ingest/chatgpt.py`) **merge** the
  human prompt and the AI reply into a single fragment whose `source.author` defaults to
  `Authorship.SELF`. `Fragment.voice_proxy_eligible` then returns `True`, so AI prose is treated
  as a voice exemplar. Human and AI text are kept separate in metadata but never attributed
  separately.
- **The de-slop pass runs but often no-ops silently.** `AIStyleConfig.enabled` defaults to
  `True` and the `creek draft` CLI path *does* wire `fingerprint` + `ai_style_config`
  (`creek/cli.py:2642`). But `DraftGenerator._apply_voice_fidelity` (`creek/generate/drafts.py`)
  **silently returns the body unchanged** when the fingerprint is `None`/thin or the config is
  disabled (`return body, {}`), and `run_voice_fidelity_guard`
  (`creek/generate/ai_style/guard.py`) only *rewrites* when distance exceeds the permissive
  `voice_distance_upper=0.35` ceiling. A mannered draft scoring just under the ceiling is
  **measured and stamped but not stripped** — consistent with the owner's suspicion.

## Open decisions (surfaced, not silently resolved)

1. **AI-turn attribution value.** The owner says AI output is "ai-assisted." The current
   `Authorship` enum is `{self, ai, other, collaborative}` — there is no `ai-assisted` value.
   **Default chosen for Issue 02:** attribute AI turns as `Authorship.AI` with `voice_weight=0.0`
   (this already excludes them from the proxy via `voice_proxy_eligible`); keep human turns as
   `Authorship.SELF`. Human chat turns then carry the existing `PERSONAL` privacy tier, so Issue
   03's audience weighting naturally ranks them below published essays. If the owner wants a
   *distinct* `ai-assisted` enum value (e.g., to mark human turns produced inside an AI session as
   lower-authority-than-essay-but-still-self), that is a one-line model addition — flagged in
   Issue 02 as a checkbox decision rather than assumed.
2. **De-slop target threshold.** Issue 05 re-examines whether `voice_distance_upper=0.35` should
   remain the *rewrite trigger*, or whether a separate, tighter **target** distance should drive
   the rewrite loop while the ceiling stays the accepted-divergence gate. Default: introduce a
   `voice_distance_target` (rewrite toward it) distinct from the ceiling; document both.
